# Chasing-Looters-2D
Try to protect your village from the constant waves of raiders

# Game Explanation
There is a guard trying to protect our village and there are waves of enemies coming at us endlessly, our main goal is to protect our Home.
Every 30 seconds enemy wave cooldown and boss comes in the 10th wave
For every enemy we kill, we can earn gold and buy soldiers or build towers.
Using the slide we can make your friends follow us or protect the Main House

We have 6 types of friends in total and we can build 6 types of towers.We can buy with merchant

Soldier-->A guardian with medium health at normal level
Peasent-->Long range but low health
Priest-->He has very little health but he provides life support to us and our friends.
Kngiht-->A protector whose life and attacks are quite appropriate
Thief-->He can catch up with any enemy very quickly, but we need to be careful, he can steal money from us.
Ally Boss-->His health and attacks are extremely high, almost worth 10 enemies.

Tower1-->Shoots arrows at normal level for the first level
Tower2-->Slightly more health and range
Tower3-->Its range is too high, its health is a bit low, its cooldown is a bit long.
Tower4-->Its range is too high and its health is too high. Its cooldown is a bit long.
Tower5-->Its range is a bit low, its health is medium, its cooldown is a bit low.
Tower6-->Its range is extremely high and its health is very high and its cooldown is very long.

There are 12 enemy types in total
Light Bandit-->An enemy with low health and damage, normal hit time and medium speed.
Heavy Bandit-->An enemy with high health and damage, normal hit time and medium speed.
Demon Bat-->An enemy with very low health and damage, normal hit time and medium speed.
Golem-->An enemy with high health and damage and a long hit time.
Flying eye-->An enemy with very low health and damage, normal hit time and medium speed.
Goblin-->An enemy with medium health and damage but low hit time.
Mushroom-->An enemy with medium health and damage but low hit time.
Skeleton-->An enemy with medium health and damage but low hit time.
Samurai-->An enemy that is fast, has a lot of damage, and has medium health.
Slime-->An enemy with very little health and damage, very slow but constantly attacking.
Spider-->An enemy with medium health and damage, medium speed but constantly attacking.
EVil Wizard(Enemy Boss)-->An enemy with extremely high health and damage, very slow and attacking with continuous fire, only comes alone

# Controls
w,a,s,d or Arrow keys to Player movement
Sprint with "Left Shift" key
Damage blocking with the "Q" key
Interact with the merchant with the "E" key
Tumbling with the "C" key
Perform 3 consecutive attack combinations with the "Fire1" key
